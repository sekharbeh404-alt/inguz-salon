About INGUZ Salon

A Modern Unisex Sanctuary for Hair & Beauty Care in Sambalpur.

Situated on the 2nd Floor of Jalan Tower near Dhaba by Kedia's, INGUZ Salon is a women-owned unisex salon and beauty studio founded and led by Shivani Sharma, welcoming both men and women. Designed with warm architectural lighting and serene, modern interiors, the studio provides a refined environment built around comfort and unhurried personal care.

Every visit is scheduled by appointment starting from 10:00 AM—ensuring focused attention whether you are coming in for haircuts and hairstyling, colour, smoothening, restorative hair treatments, skincare, professional makeup, or nail and foot grooming.
